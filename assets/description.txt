Dibbler Escape

Dibbler Escape is a cartoony browser game born from a very real feeling: stepping out of the hotel on a tourist island and realizing, a few blocks in, that every street vendor within sight has noticed you. You play a tourist in a loud Hawaiian shirt trying to make it back to the hotel through a maze of winding streets, while a growing crowd of pushy merchants, the dibblers, close in from every side. They have view cones, they shout to alert each other, and when they lose you they huddle up in a "group chat" to work out where you went. Get caught and you have to throw money at them to get away. Run out of cash, and it's game over.

It's a game about the quiet panic of the introvert abroad, played for laughs. You can sprint, but only until you run out of stamina. You can pet a lucky cat for a tip, or kick your overly friendly travel buddy to make a noise and distract the crowd. Each level brings more dibblers, and the safety of the hotel door has never felt so far away. It's plain HTML5 canvas with no installs and no build step, so it runs in any browser, on desktop or touch. Play it and see how far you get before the "Excuse me, sir, just one minute!" starts.
